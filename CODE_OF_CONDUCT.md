# Code of Conduct

## Our commitment

The project aims to provide a respectful, constructive space for people with different backgrounds, experience levels, and perspectives. Participation in issues, discussions, reviews, and contributions is expected to be professional and considerate.

## Expected behavior

- Be respectful and constructive.
- Focus criticism on ideas, implementation, and evidence rather than individuals.
- Accept responsibility for mistakes and correct them in good faith.
- Share context that helps others understand a technical decision.
- Respect privacy and do not publish another person's private information.
- Keep feedback actionable and relevant to the project.

## Unacceptable behavior

- Harassment, discrimination, threats, or personal attacks.
- Deliberate intimidation or sustained disruptive behavior.
- Publishing private information without permission.
- Sexualized language or unwelcome advances.
- Trolling, insults, or abusive comments in issues, reviews, or discussions.
- Knowingly sharing credentials, private interview content, or other sensitive data.

## Reporting

Report conduct concerns privately to the repository maintainer, Brajesh Kumar, through the maintainer's GitHub profile: https://github.com/Brajesh31. Do not include sensitive personal data in a public issue.

## Enforcement

The maintainer may edit or remove harmful content, close discussions, request corrective behavior, restrict participation, or block accounts where necessary to protect contributors and users. Decisions should be proportionate to the behavior and the safety of the community.

## Scope

This code applies to project spaces, including issues, pull requests, code reviews, discussions, and project-related public communication.
